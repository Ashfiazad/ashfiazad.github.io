About the Artist

Born in 1990, Hafsa Binta Azad is a contemporary painter known for their evocative abstract works. Their unique approach blends traditional techniques with modern themes, exploring the intersection of nature and urban life.

With a background in fine arts from the Royal College of Art, their work has been exhibited internationally and featured in numerous prestigious collections.
